FAQ

Questions broke people ask

What even is this?

No Money turns "I'm broke" into a shareable page. Pick your broke status, drop your tip links, and post a meme-ready card with a Broke Score. People pity you (financially).

Is it free?

Yes — creating and sharing a page is free, no signup.

Do you take a cut of my tips?

Never. Your links (PayPal, Ko-fi, crypto…) go straight to you — No Money never touches the money.

Do I need an account?

Nope. Your page is just a shareable link — no login, no password to forget.

Is this a real fundraising or charity thing?

No. It's for personal support pages, creator tips and internet jokes — not charity or fundraising. Be broke responsibly.
